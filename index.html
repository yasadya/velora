Made by Hand, Made with Love

Made Slowly.
Made With Intention.

Every Velora bar begins on a small wooden table in Sri Lanka — lye and oils, a whipped soap "frosting," and roses piped one petal at a time. Nothing is rushed.

We believe self-care should feel like a little ceremony, not another chore. So we pour small batches, cure them slowly, and finish each bar with skin-loving actives — kojic acid, niacinamide, cocoa, honey — wrapped in scents that feel like a garden after rain.

The Velora Process

How a Bar Comes to Life

01

Choose

We measure lye, water and thoughtfully selected oils.

02

Blend

Oils and lye are carefully combined by hand until smooth.

03

Pour

Colour, scent and small batches are poured into the mould.

04

Pipe

We whip soap into frosting and piece together tiny roses, by hand.

05

Cure

Bars rest 24–48 hours, then cure for 4–6 weeks to soften beautifully.

06

Wrap

Finished, labelled and wrapped with love, ready for your ritual.
